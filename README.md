
## Amirreza Mirzaei

Undergraduate in Microbiology with a secondary major in Mathematics and Applications, University of Tehran. Working toward graduate research in computational neuroscience and machine learning.

My interest sits at the intersection of biological information processing and formal inference. Living systems solve estimation and decision problems under uncertainty long before nervous systems exist — bacterial chemotaxis approximates gradient ascent on noisy signals, and bet-hedging strategies in microbial populations implement something close to portfolio optimization. I came to computational neuroscience through that lens, and I'm building the mathematical and computational foundations to work on it seriously.

**Current focus**
- Probability and statistical inference, with emphasis on Bayesian methods
- Linear algebra and its computational implementation
- Python for scientific computing (foundations first: semantics, data structures, algorithmic reasoning)
